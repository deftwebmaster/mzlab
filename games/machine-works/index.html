Bring the line back to life

Drill digs ore → belts carry it → Smelter turns 2 ore into a plate → the Sink ships it. Machines push out the way they face and need Power.

  • Build pick a tool, then tap a tile. Drag to lay a run of belts.
  • Rotate R or the Rotate button, before or after placing.
  • Remove long-press or right-click a piece. Ctrl+Z undoes.
  • Look around drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom, double-tap to centre.

One drill feeds half a smelter. Fill each order to unlock a bigger one — the starter line will not keep up for long.
